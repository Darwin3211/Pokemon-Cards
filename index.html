Pokemon Trainers

Ash Ketchum

Ash Ketchm is a trainer from Palette Town that aspire to become a Pokemon master, his first pokemon is Pikachu not the one of the beginner trio

Gray Oak

Gray Oak Gary Oak is the grandson of Professor Samuel Oak and a talented Pokémon Trainer from Pallet Town. He is known for being arrogant, confident, and competitive, especially toward his rival, Ash Ketchum. As he grows older, Gary becomes more mature and eventually follows his grandfather's footsteps by becoming a Pokémon researcher.

Brandon

Brandon is a powerful Pokémon Trainer known as the leader of the Battle Pyramid in the Hoenn region's Battle Frontier. He is an experienced battler who specializes in using Legendary Pokémon.

May

May is a Pokémon Trainer from Petalburg City who travels with Ash and his friends. She begins her journey as a beginner but eventually becomes a skilled Pokémon Coordinator who competes in Pokémon Contests.

Steven Stone

Steven Stone is the former Champion of the Hoenn region and the son of the president of the Devon Corporation. He is a powerful Trainer who specializes in Steel-type Pokémon and has a strong interest in rare stones and minerals.

Giovanni

Giovanni is the leader of Team Rocket, a criminal organization that seeks to exploit Pokémon for its own purposes. He is also the Gym Leader of Viridian City and is known for using powerful Ground-type Pokémon.
